Under the hood

Three agents,
one studio.

Each agent has a single job. Together they produce posters that preserve your product, read like good copy, and land like real design.

01

Image Agent

SAM-HQ segments your product with pixel precision. Stable Diffusion inpaints a coherent background. QA checks integrity, artifacts, style.

02

Text Agent

Reads your product with vision AI — type, material, color, style — then writes a headline, description, and CTA in the tone you choose.

03

Layout Agent

Assembles the final poster with a PosterO-style layout tree — balancing type, product, and negative space for different platforms.

✦

Product preservation

The core promise: the system builds around your real product — never replaces or warps it.
